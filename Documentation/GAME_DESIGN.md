# Game Design — JANTAR MANTAR RUN: Delhi Chase

## High concept
A mobile third-person endless runner set in a fictional, comedic Delhi streetscape inspired by the Jantar Mantar area. The player runs through a stylized road while avoiding traffic and obstacles as police officers pursue them. This is fictional action-comedy, not a recreation of a real incident.

## Opening sequence
1. Establish the road, city dressing, and metal barricades.
2. The player character enters the road and shouts a short slogan such as “Jai Hind” or “Inquilab Zindabad”.
3. Police begin chasing from behind; occasional scripted appearances ahead create surprises.
4. Transition into the endless-run gameplay loop.

Use original or licensed audio. Add user-supplied meme clips only after confirming permission to use them.

## Core gameplay
- Three lanes: left, center, right.
- Actions: change lane, jump, slide.
- Obstacles: barricades, parked or moving vehicles, and readable road hazards.
- Optional coins and score.
- Police chase pressure escalates over time.
- End condition: police catch the player. Keep the detention sequence brief, stylized, and non-graphic.

## End sequence
1. Stop player control and play a short detention animation.
2. Transition the character into a police transport vehicle.
3. Show Game Over and a Restart option.
4. Restart resets score, coins, obstacle state, and chase state.

## Minimum playable prototype
- One greybox road/environment segment.
- Placeholder player and basic run/jump/slide animations.
- Three-lane movement and camera follow.
- Obstacles, collision checks, and repeating road segments.
- Simple police chase/caught state.
- Game Over and restart.
- Android touch input.

## Later additions
Polished environment, approved character/vehicle assets, licensed meme audio, surprise events, missions, settings, accessibility, and performance polish.

## Design principles
Keep controls forgiving and obstacles readable. Avoid unfair unavoidable collisions. Use fictionalized comedy and do not present invented events as factual.
